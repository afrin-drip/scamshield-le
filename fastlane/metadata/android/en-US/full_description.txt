ScamShield LE is your intelligent, always-on guardian against phone scams and fraudulent callers.

Powered by advanced AI, ScamShield LE analyzes incoming calls in real time and instantly alerts you when suspicious scam patterns are detected, before you lose a single dollar or share any personal information.

KEY FEATURES

Real-Time AI Detection
ScamShield LE listens during live calls and uses on-device AI analysis to identify common scam techniques including impersonation, urgency pressure tactics, and requests for personal or financial information.

Instant Alerts
Receive immediate notifications the moment a potential scam is detected, giving you the power to hang up and stay safe.

Scam Call History
Every flagged call is logged with details so you can review past threats and build awareness of scam patterns targeting you.

Runs in the Background
ScamShield LE works silently without interrupting your daily routine. It activates automatically when calls come in.

Privacy First
Your audio and call data are processed entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded, shared, or stored in the cloud.

PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED
Phone: Required to detect when a call is incoming and to monitor the call state.
Microphone: Required to analyze the audio of live calls for scam indicators.
Notifications: Required to send you real-time scam alerts.
